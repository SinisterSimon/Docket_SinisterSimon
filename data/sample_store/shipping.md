# Shipping

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 14:00 (Tallinn time) on a working day leave our warehouse the same day.
Delivery to Estonia takes 1–2 working days, to Latvia and Lithuania 2–3 working days, and to the rest of the EU 3–6 working days.

## Shipping costs
Shipping within Estonia costs 4.90 EUR and is free on orders over 100 EUR.
Shipping to other EU countries costs 9.90 EUR and is free on orders over 200 EUR.
We do not ship outside the EU.

## Tracking
Every order gets a tracking link by email as soon as it leaves the warehouse.
If the tracking link has not updated for 3 working days, contact us and we will check with the carrier.
