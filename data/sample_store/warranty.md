# Warranty

## What is covered
All boards have a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects: core delamination, edge separation and topsheet cracks not caused by impact.

## What is not covered
Damage from rocks, rails, crashes, improper mounting or normal wear is not covered.

## How to claim
Email photos of the damage and your order number. We reply within 2 working days and, if approved, repair or replace the board.
